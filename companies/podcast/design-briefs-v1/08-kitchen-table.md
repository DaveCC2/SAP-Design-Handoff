# 08 · 1h — Kitchen Table

## Feeling

Human before institutional. Someone is listening to you rather than addressing you — warm, close, slightly unglamorous on purpose.

## Layout logic

Photo left, words right, vertically centred against each other. The classic split, weighted 44/56 so the type stays the subject and the photograph stays the company.

## Key visual choice

The photograph bleeds off three edges with no frame, no rounding, no scrim, no tint — a listening frame rather than a promotional one. Nothing is done to the face at all.

# 07 · 1g — Transcript

## Feeling

You are already mid-conversation. Nothing is being marketed to you; you have opened a document of people talking.

## Layout logic

A timecode gutter on the left, a single speaking column on the right. The sentence is set as Jerremy's opening line; the proof slot is Dave answering. The button is the next turn in the exchange.

## Key visual choice

Speaker labels in two different accent colours. The two registers are structural rather than pictorial — you can see there are two people before you meet either face.

## Spec

```
Ground: #fcfcfa
Gutter: 132px, border-right 1px #e4e2da, timecodes IBM Plex Mono 400 10px/.14em #6a675f at 00:00:00 / 00:00:06 / 00:00:41 / 00:01:12
Speaker labels: IBM Plex Mono 600 10px/.2em — JERREMY #b4551f, DAVE #2f6f5e
H1: Newsreader 400, 32px/1.32, -.012em, #16150f (set as Jerremy's line)
Button: solid #16150f
Button copy: Subscribe — wherever you listen
```

## Honest risk

Most original structure in the set. Asks the visitor to understand a convention before they understand the show.

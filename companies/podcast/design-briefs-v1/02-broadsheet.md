# 02 · 1b — Broadsheet

## Feeling

A paper of record, not a channel. Serious on arrival — earned authority before a single claim is made.

## Layout logic

Masthead, standing head, then the sentence as the front-page lead. The ask is pinned to a bottom rule like a subscription line, so the page reads top-to-bottom exactly like a front page.

## Key visual choice

Hairline rules and a standing head replace the navigation bar entirely. Cadence, self-funded and no-party sit in the dateline strip where a paper puts its edition.

## Spec

```
Ground: #f7f5f0 · Ink: #12100e
Masthead: 3px top rule, 1px bottom rule, wordmark Newsreader 600 24px/.22em centred; cover tile 46px beneath
Dateline strip: IBM Plex Mono 400 10px/.1em #5c584f, 1px rule below — NEW EPISODES MON–THU · SELF-FUNDED · UNBOUGHT · NO PARTY AFFILIATION
H1: Newsreader 400, 41px/1.14, -.018em
Footer band: 1px rule, proof slot left, button right
Button: solid #12100e on #f7f5f0
Button copy: Subscribe — wherever you listen
```

## Honest risk

Strongest credibility signal in the set. Risk: reads as journalism, and the show is not journalism.

# 01 · 1a — Sunday Night

## Feeling

The lamp still on at nine on a Sunday. Quiet, adult, unhurried — the room where you actually give something an hour.

## Layout logic

One centred column, vertically centred, nothing in the corners. No nav, no rail, no second column. The eye has one path: name, sentence, proof, button.

## Key visual choice

A single warm pool of light behind the type instead of any image. The page is lit, not decorated — the only bright object on screen is the button.

## Spec

```
Ground: #0b0d10, with radial-gradient(110% 75% at 50% 6%, rgba(224,164,88,.18), transparent 62%)
H1: Newsreader 300, 40px/1.24, -.015em, #f6f3ec, centred
Wordmark: IBM Plex Mono 500, 10px, .28em, #9c968a — cover tile 38px beside it
Button: #e0a458 ground, #14161a text, Public Sans 700 15px, 17px/30px pad
Measure: centred column, 130px side padding
Button copy: Subscribe — wherever you listen
```

## Honest risk

Warmest and quietest of the set. Least distinctive at thumbnail size — it lives or dies on the sentence.

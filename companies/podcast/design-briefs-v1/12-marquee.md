# 12 · 1l — Marquee

## Feeling

Broadcast confidence. Loud, certain, a show that expects an audience — the highest-volume option, and the biggest risk of reading as pundit television.

## Layout logic

Three horizontal bands with nothing centred: ticker, name, sentence at maximum scale, then the ask. The type fills the frame to the margins so the fold feels like a title card.

## Key visual choice

A ticker strip carries the standing facts and the proof line, which frees the hero of every secondary element.

## Spec

```
Ground: #0e0e0f
Ticker: full-bleed #dcae4a band, 9px pad, IBM Plex Mono 500 10px/.16em #141414, marquee overflow (clipping intentional)
H1: Archivo 800, 46px/1.02, -.035em, #f2f0ea
Wordmark: IBM Plex Mono 500 10px/.24em #a5a29a; cover tile 36px
Button: transparent, 2px #dcae4a border, #dcae4a text, Archivo 800 16px
Button copy: Subscribe — wherever you listen
```

## Honest risk

Honest stress test: does this energy read as a movement, or as cable news? Highest tonal risk in the set.

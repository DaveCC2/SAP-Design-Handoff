# 13 · 1m — Field Notes

## Feeling

Working papers left open on the desk. Rigorous, unfinished, thinking in public — the producer's side of the show made visible.

## Layout logic

A wide main measure with a narrow annotation rail down the right, divided by a single hairline. The sentence and the button own the main column; nothing in the rail competes for the click.

## Key visual choice

Numbered marginalia in monospace beside the pitch. The claim and its footnotes share the fold, which is how a serious reader is flattered without being lectured.

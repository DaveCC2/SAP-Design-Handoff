# 03 · 1c — Two Voices

## Feeling

Two people who disagree in public and stay in the room. Tension, not harmony — the thing that makes the show unlike a panel.

## Layout logic

A hard 50/50 vertical split, one host per half, with the sentence floated on a cream card straddling the seam. The button lives on the card, so the ask sits on neither side.

## Key visual choice

The two halves are graded to different colour temperatures — warm left, cool right. The register difference the stone describes in words is stated in light before a word is read.

# 11 · 1k — No Jersey

## Feeling

Unaffiliated and calm about it. Cool, clean, almost clinical — the visual equivalent of refusing to pick a side out loud.

## Layout logic

Strict central axis with air on all four sides and the two standing facts pinned to the top and bottom edges. Symmetry does the work of neutrality.

## Key visual choice

One accent for the entire page, deliberately neither red nor blue. A deep green carries the only colour on screen, and the palette states the position before the copy does.

## Spec

```
Ground: #ffffff · Ink: #111111 · Sole accent: #2f6f5e
Rule row: IBM Plex Mono 500 10px/.2em #2f6f5e, 34px hairlines either side — NO RED. NO BLUE.
H1: Public Sans 300, 36px/1.3, -.018em, centred
Standing facts (bottom): IBM Plex Mono 400 10px #67645c — FREE · SELF-FUNDED · NO PARTY AFFILIATION
Button: #2f6f5e ground, #ffffff text
Button copy: Subscribe — wherever you listen
>>> NO COVER ART. The supplied artwork is a red-white-and-blue flag relief; placing it here negates the
    direction's entire argument. If 1k wins, it needs a mark that is not the flag — a wordmark or monogram.
```

## Honest risk

Sharpest strategic statement in the set. Requires new brand work to ship.

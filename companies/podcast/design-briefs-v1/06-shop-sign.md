# 06 · 1f — Shop Sign

## Feeling

Painted signage. Confident, plain-spoken, legible from across the street — 'shop sign English' taken literally.

## Layout logic

Type only, edge to edge, three tiers: name, sign, ask. Nothing is centred and nothing is decorative — a flat painted surface with words on it.

## Key visual choice

The only direction that splits the sentence: a 12-word sign at 52px with the full pitch carried verbatim underneath. The deliberate test case for whether the sentence needs shortening.

## Spec

```
Ground: #1d3a34 · Type: #f4efe4
Sign line: Archivo 800, 52px/.98, -.03em — "Real conversations about America's toughest issues, turned into a movement."
Full pitch beneath: Public Sans 400, 15px/1.55, #bfd6cf, max 62ch
Wordmark: IBM Plex Mono 500 10px/.22em #8db3a8; cover tile 38px right
Button: #f4efe4 ground, #14261f text, Archivo 800 16px, 19px/32px pad
Button copy: Subscribe — wherever you listen
```

## Honest risk

THE SENTENCE TEST. If Dave wants the pitch shortened, this is the control. Compare against 1a, which uses it whole.

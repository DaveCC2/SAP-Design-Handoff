# 10 · 1j — Index

## Feeling

A body of work, not a launch. Fifty-odd interviews deep and organised — research-library restraint rather than promotion.

## Layout logic

Sentence and button in one band across the top, proof as a ruled table beneath. The only direction where the proof strip is visible above the fold rather than promised below it.

## Key visual choice

Heavy numerals in pale grey as the dominant visual rhythm. Depth is drawn with counting, and the empty slots read honestly as an index waiting on its entries.

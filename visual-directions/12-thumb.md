## Layout

Vertical story, in this order, no shortcuts:

1. Wordmark
2. Sentence
3. Second breath
4. Proof line
5. Snapping proof cards (stack)
6. Manifesto
7. Dive-deeper (one quiet door)
8. Act
9. FAQ
10. Two bios

**The key move:** a sticky subscribe bar at the bottom of the viewport. The button never leaves her thumb. `min-height: 44px`. Safe-area aware. The in-page hero does **not** duplicate a second competing button — the sticky bar *is* the button. (A single in-flow button is allowed only if the sticky bar is the same control, not a different CTA.)

## Key visual

A sticky subscribe bar. That's the whole argument. White field, black type, red button. No decoration that steals the thumb.

## Hard fail if

- Desktop becomes a multi-column marketing site.
- The sticky bar hides the act or the FAQ with no way to reach them.
- Two different CTAs (Subscribe vs Join vs Donate).

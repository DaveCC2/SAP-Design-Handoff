## Feeling

If she can't say it back, the page failed. No atmosphere to hide in. The sentence is the product. Everything else is a footnote she can scroll to.

3PM: I can repeat this to a friend.  
3AM (unnamed): this is not noise.

---

## Layout

- The elevator pitch **is** the page. Giant type, four or five lines, the button as the period.
- First viewport: wordmark (small), the sentence (architectural), the button, platforms. Stop.
- Everything else — proof, manifesto, dive-deeper, act, FAQ, hosts — is a footnote band with smaller type and more air.

## Key visual

Type as architecture. No image in the first viewport. Size that fills a phone. Tracking tight enough to feel like a shop sign, not a paragraph. Break the sentence on meaning, not on a character count.

Suggested break:

```
The weekly podcast
that turns real
conversations about
America's toughest
issues into a
movement.
```

Do not break on "America's" in a way that orphans "Problems." Do not set it as a justified block.

## Copy on this page

- H1: the sentence, and only the sentence.
- Button: `Start listening` — visually the period.
- Deck lives below the fold if at all.

## Story spine

1. Listen — the sentence *is* step one.
2. Trust — footnotes, not a gallery.
3. Act — a footnote with weight.

## Assets

No photographs above the fold. Wordmark small, top-left or top-center. Footer portraits only.

## Hard fail if

- A background image, texture pack, or flag wash appears behind the type.
- The sentence is rewritten to fit the type.
- A second headline is added ("Welcome to…").

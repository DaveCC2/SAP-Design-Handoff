## Feeling

The room she aspires to walk into. Elizabeth McCord energy without naming the character. Paper, ink, one photograph, a thin column of deck. She is being briefed, not converted.

3PM: this will not waste the hour.  
3AM (unnamed): there is still a serious room left in public life.

---

## Layout

- Magazine cover, not a website template.
- Wordmark as the masthead, small, letterspaced.
- One large serif headline (the sentence, broken like a cover line).
- One photograph — a host portrait that looks sat-for, not a thumbnail. Prefer Jerremy contemplative or sitting, not the grin.
- A thin column of deck beside or under the photo.
- Subscribe as a small red rule plus a word (`Subscribe`), not a fat pill.
- FAQ as briefing notes — Q as a label, A as the note. Not an accordion support widget.
- Proof, act, hosts follow like inside pages.

## Key visual

Warm paper and ink. Not navy-and-gold consulting. Paper, not screen. Portrait lighting. Generous margins. A hairline rule. If you need a date-line, use a day, not a volume number that pretends you are *The Atlantic*.

## Story spine

## Assets

One portrait in the cover. The other host appears inside, later. Do not make a two-shot cover (that's 03).

## Hard fail if

- Gold foil, navy suiting, think-tank annual report.
- It becomes *Politico* or *The Economist* cosplay.
- A carnival of cover lines.

# 01 · Sunday Night

Load `_shared.md` first.

**Chip:** `#c4a574`  
**Register:** Quiet. Lamp, not rally. Jill's actual listening hour.

---

## Feeling

The quiet hour before the week. She can sit with this without putting on a costume. Civic duty does not have to look like a costume other people wore. The page should feel like a room she already has, not a hall she is being invited to enter.

3PM: a conversation worth the hour, no noise.  
3AM (unnamed): she is not a spectator tonight.

---

## Layout

- Narrow reading column (~34rem) in a dark field.
- Hero is the sentence + one button. Nothing else competes.
- Proof arrives as a **slow vertical list**, not a carousel.
- Manifesto, dive-deeper, and the weekly act wait until she has decided to stay.
- Hosts in the footer. Equal, not a hero portrait.
- Desktop: same column, more margin. Do not widen the type into a billboard.

## Key visual

Warm 2700K light on near-black. One lamp. A radial wash of lamp-gold, not studio glare. No flag. No marble. No waveform.

## Palette and type

| Token | Value |
|---|---|
| Background | `#0c0a07` |
| Foreground | `#f3ead8` |
| Accent | `#c4a574` (lamp, not logo-red) |
| Type | Newsreader / Georgia. Reading serif. Size ~17px body, H1 ~2.05–2.4rem, medium weight, generous leading. |

Logo-red (`#c8102e`) may live inside the wordmark. Do not use it as the page accent here — that would turn the lamp into a rally.

## Copy on this page

- Eyebrow, optional: `Sunday night`
- H1: the sentence (locked)
- Deck: the second breath (locked)
- Button: `Start listening`
- Platforms as a whisper under the button
- Proof as stacked slots: A CEO / An everyday person / An expert

Do not write "the country is dying." Do not write "join the movement" as a shout.

## Story spine

1. Listen — sentence in the lamp-lit column.
2. Trust — three stacked empty guest cards. Honesty of the blank.
3. Act — one quiet block, after the manifesto.

## Assets

Wordmark on dark. Headshots only in the footer, if at all. Prefer the quieter frames (contemplative, sitting) over the grin.

## Hard fail if

- It looks like a campaign launch or a TED stage.
- Gold becomes luxury-brand gold.
- The column gets replaced by a full-bleed image.

## Not these other directions

Not 07 (volume). Not 09 (play mark). Not 12 (sticky thumb bar). This one earns the hour by being still.

**Register:** Stranger on the sidewalk. No mystique.

---

## Feeling

A stranger gets it without scrolling. Painted on the glass. No brochure, no atmospheric copy, no "who we are" paragraph before the name. If she cannot read it from across the street, it failed.

3PM: I know what this is in one look.  
3AM (unnamed): this is not another costume.

---

## Layout

- Wordmark **is** the sign. Large, top, unapologetic.
- The sentence stacked like wood type — short lines, not a paragraph block.
- The button sits under it like the door.
- Proof is a **ticker or strip below the fold only**. Nothing photographic above the fold.
- FAQ, manifesto, act, hosts: downstairs.

## Key visual

The logo's red used only on PROBLEMS (inside the wordmark) and the button. Everything else is black and white. Type as paint. No photographs above the fold. No gradients. No glassmorphism.

## Palette and type

| Token | Value |

## Copy on this page

- Wordmark as identity; do not add a second brand line above it.
- H1: the sentence, stacked.
- Button: `Subscribe — wherever you listen`
- No deck above the fold. The sign does not explain itself.

## Story spine

1. Listen — sign + sentence + door.
2. Trust — proof ticker after she has walked in.
3. Act — a posted notice, not a campaign form.

## Assets

Wordmark on a black chip if the JPEG is reverse; otherwise the tight wordmark. No headshots in the hero. Footer portraits only.

## Hard fail if

- A photo appears above the fold.
- Red leaks into backgrounds, rules, or "accent sections."
- It starts looking like a restaurant menu or a zine cover (that's 06).

## Not these other directions

Not 04 (the sentence *is* the architecture; here the *wordmark* is). Not 10 (no civic rule). Not 14 (14 removes color; 02 uses the red as paint).

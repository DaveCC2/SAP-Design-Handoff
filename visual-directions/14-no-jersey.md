# 14 · No Jersey

Load `_shared.md` first.

**Chip:** `#cfc8be`  
**Register:** Relief. Nobody is going to make you pick a team.

---

## Feeling

Shoulders drop. The anti-tribalism is the design. Absence as brand. She has been braced for a jersey since the headline; this page refuses to hand her one.

3PM: no team, no lecture.  
3AM (unnamed): I can participate without becoming a type.

---

## Layout

- Neutral field. Low contrast, not washed out.
- FAQ pulled **up** next to the sentence as a quiet caption — especially `What party? Undetermined.`
- Button is **outlined, not filled.** An invitation, not a conversion.
- Proof, manifesto, act follow without colored section bands.
- The most restrained page of the fifteen. If you want to add a thing, don't.

## Key visual

No blue at all. Red only inside the wordmark's PROBLEMS. No filled red button. No red rule. Hairline borders, stone-grey type, paper ground. The outlined button is the only interactive object above the fold besides the wordmark.

## Copy on this page

- H1: the sentence
- Immediate caption: the `What party?` FAQ, locked
- Then the other two FAQs, still near the top
- Button: `Start listening` (ghost)
- Proof line after the FAQs, not before — relief first, range second

Do not add "independent" as a party. Undetermined means undetermined.

## Story spine

1. Listen — sentence + ghost button.
2. Trust — the FAQ *is* the trust (we will not jersey you), then proof slots.
3. Act — still present, still quiet.

## Assets

Wordmark on a black chip so PROBLEMS can stay red without painting the page. No hero photo. Hosts in footer, unstyled.

## Hard fail if

- Any blue. Any red fill. Any star, stripe, donkey, elephant.
- A "both sides" graphic.
- The outlined button becoming a filled button "for conversion."

## Not these other directions

Not 10 (10 uses red as a rule; 14 hides it). Not 07 (slam vs relief). Not 02 (02 paints with red; 14 withholds it).

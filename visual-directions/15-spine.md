**01 Listen**  
The sentence. The second breath. One subscribe button. Platforms. Stop. No guests yet. No manifesto yet.

**02 Trust**

The specific thing, framed `When you're ready` + `this week`. FILL for the act. Not a donate form. Not a petition widget.

After 03: FAQ and hosts as the last page, un-numbered.

## Feeling

Jerremy's optimism and Dave's skepticism are visibly in tension. That tension *is* the show. A blended "podcast voice" is a failure. She should be able to tell who is who without reading the names.

3PM: two adults, not a pundit.  
3AM (unnamed): disagreement is allowed; typecasting is not.

---

## Layout

- Two-column from the first fold.
- **Left / Jerremy / hot:** larger type, warmer ground, heart. Pull from his short bio: talks like he runs, full speed, heart first.
- **Right / Dave / cold:** tighter type, cooler ground, mechanism. Pull from his short bio: tech, systems, public service — do not editorialize.
- A shared **button-band** stitches them: one subscribe control spanning both columns.
- On a phone: Jerremy, then Dave, then the stitch. Do not interleave sentences.
- Above the columns: the sentence as a shared roof.
- Below: proof, manifesto, act, FAQ. Hosts can be omitted from a second footer if the columns already carried the bios — or repeat them quietly. Do not add a third introduction.

## Key visual

The page has two temperatures. Warm paper vs a cooler grey-tan. Jerremy in a display serif (Fraunces). Dave in a tighter sans (Outfit). Photographs at the top of each column, different crops, same dignity. Labels: `Heart first` and `The mechanism` — not "good cop / bad cop," not "vision / operations" as a rank.
